# Spin & Learn — Classroom Reward Spinner

**Paste a word list. Ask. Spin. Earn stars.**

Spin & Learn is a classroom reward spinner built for ESL teachers, tutors, and homeschool parents. You paste a list of words or questions, ask a question out loud, and when a learner answers correctly, the class spins the wheel and earns stars. It's a classroom reward tool, not a game of chance: learners earn stars for correct answers, nothing more.

## How to use it (3 steps)

1. **Pick a pack or paste your own list.** Open the Word List panel, choose one of the built-in packs (ESL Basic, Math, or Blank), or paste your own lines as `question | answer | hint`.
2. **Ask a question.** Show the card to the class and call on a learner to answer.
3. **Spin for stars.** A correct answer earns a spin. Spin the wheel, watch the class earn stars, and build a streak.

- Any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
- Works **offline** — no internet needed in the classroom.
- No login, no installation, no student accounts, no student data collected.
