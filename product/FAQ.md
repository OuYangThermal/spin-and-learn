# Frequently Asked Questions — Spin & Learn

**1. Does it work offline?**
Yes. The paid download is a single standalone HTML file — double-click it and it runs with no internet connection.

**2. Does it work on a smart whiteboard?**
Yes. Any smart whiteboard with a modern browser can run it. The online demo works in the browser; the paid download works even when the classroom has no internet.

**3. Do students need accounts or names?**
No. There are no accounts, no logins, and no names to enter. It's a whole-class game run by the teacher.

**4. What student data is collected?**
None. The game stores nothing about students — no names, no scores on a server, no tracking. Everything happens in your browser.

**5. How do I add my own words?**
Open the Word List panel and paste one item per line in this format:
`question | answer | hint`
The question part is required; answer and hint are optional. Example: `apple | 苹果 | a round red fruit`

**6. Can I import a CSV?**
Yes. Import/export is included in the paid download. Save your lists as files and reuse them across classes and school years. The free online demo does not include import/export.

**7. Can I share the game with other teachers?**
The free online demo can be shared with anyone. The paid download is licensed to the purchaser (one teacher or one family) — if a colleague wants the downloadable version, they'll need their own copy.

**8. Can I use it commercially (tutoring business, paid classes)?**
Running the game in your own paid classes is fine under the Personal license — it's classroom use. If you want to modify the game and publish or sell your own version of it, you need the $29 Commercial/Creator License.

**9. Is internet needed in the classroom?**
Only for the free online demo. The paid download works fully offline.

**10. What's the difference between the free demo and the paid download?**
The free demo lets you play in a browser with the 3 built-in packs. The $9 Personal download adds: offline use from a double-click, the printable teacher guide, import/export, and all v0.x pack updates. The $29 Commercial download adds creator rights — modify and sell your own derivative games.

**11. Are more word packs coming?**
Paid buyers get all v0.x content pack updates at no extra charge. Future theme packs (Animals, Phonics, Multiplication, and more) will be added as they're ready.

**12. What about refunds?**
If the download doesn't run in your browser as described, contact support for help or a refund — we'd rather fix it or make it right.
