Spin & Learn — Classroom Reward Spinner

Paste a word list. Ask. Spin. Earn stars.

1

Pick a list

Open the Word List panel. Choose ESL Basic, Math, or Blank — or paste your own words (see format below).

2

Ask the class

Show a card, call on a learner, let them answer. Press Show Answer whenever you like.

3

Spin for stars

Correct answer? Press Correct ✓ — the wheel spins, the class earns stars and builds a streak. That's the whole game.

Word-list format (one item per line)

apple | 苹果 | a round red fruit 7 + 5 | 12 | count up from 7

Fields are separated by |. question is required; answer and hint are optional. Blank lines are ignored.

5 classroom tips

  1. Warm-up first: open class with 5 quick cards before the lesson.
  2. Team streaks: split the class into teams and compare star counts.
  3. Student caller: let a learner run the spins — engagement doubles.
  4. Recycle lists: export your list and reuse it for Friday review.
  5. Hint first: read the hint before revealing the answer for weaker groups.
