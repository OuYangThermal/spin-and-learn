# Spin & Learn — Classroom Reward Spinner

**Paste a word list. Ask. Spin. Earn stars.**

Your class already knows the answers. What they need is a reason to shout them out. Spin & Learn turns vocabulary review, math drills, and warm-up quizzes into a whole-class reward game: you ask the question, a learner answers, and on a correct answer the class spins the wheel and earns stars.

## Who it's for

- ESL / EFL teachers who need fast, repeatable review games that work with any word list
- Tutors and language coaches running 1-on-1 or small-group sessions
- Homeschool parents turning spelling, vocabulary, or math practice into play

## The problem it solves

Review games teachers actually reuse are rare. Most digital games lock you into fixed content you can't change; physical spinners take class time to set up and wear out. Spin & Learn is the opposite: it takes *your* words — today's vocabulary, tomorrow's quiz, next week's review list — and wraps them in a reward loop students ask for again.

## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Pick a pack or paste your list.** Choose ESL Basic, Math, or Blank, or paste your own lines as `question | answer | hint` — one per line, done in seconds.
2. **Ask the class.** Show the card, call on a learner, reveal the answer when you like.
3. **Spin for stars.** Correct answer? Spin the wheel. The class earns stars and builds a streak.

## What's included

- The complete game as **one standalone HTML file** — double-click and it runs in any modern browser, **offline**, no login, no installation

This is a classroom reward tool, not a game of chance: learners earn stars for correct answers, nothing more.
